# Competitive presence review: 360 Labs and Veyrn Labs

Reviewed 12 September 2026. Public website, portfolio, research, LinkedIn, and GitHub. Competitor statements are self-published claims, not independently verified delivery results. This is a qualitative positioning review, not an assessment of traffic, conversion, or actual engineering quality.

## What creates the impression of capability

360 Labs uses oversized typography, generous whitespace, and a broad navigation structure. Its homepage identifies a product studio and engineering lab, while dedicated destinations separate client work, products, research, team, and buying questions. This gives visitors multiple ways to investigate the company. [Homepage](https://www.360labs.ai/)

The portfolio organizes projects by sector and marks work as live, in development, or prototype. Descriptions explain concrete systems and contributions. The important lesson is to distinguish delivered work from experiments and make the scope inspectable. The volume and client claims remain self-reported. [Portfolio](https://www.360labs.ai/portfolio)

Research has its own destination, and public repositories provide inspectable technical artifacts. Veyrn can develop this layer through approved engineering write-ups and useful public tools. [Research](https://www.360labs.ai/research), [GitHub](https://github.com/360Labs-ai)

The LinkedIn page reinforces the engineering-and-research positioning. The website also links YouTube, X, and Instagram; those account activity levels and engagement were not audited. No traffic or audience-size comparisons are asserted. [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lightyear-360-labs)

Their FAQ covers services, integration, pricing, timelines, and support. This is useful buying information, although self-ranking language and sweeping promises should not be treated as independent evidence. [FAQ](https://www.360labs.ai/faq)

## Veyrn's positioning opportunity

Lead with the actual offer: AI engineering and connected business software. Support it with the strongest available distinction: policy validation before business actions, workflow-specific evaluation, human control, and direct engineering ownership.

The earlier page gave too much space to explaining studio size and too little to the substance of delivered work. Repeated text sections made everything look equally important. The inactive contact form also let visitors reach a predictable error.

A professional presentation should help a buyer assess fit, engineering judgment, and engagement clarity. It cannot establish delivery quality without supporting artifacts.

## Implemented in this revision

- Repositioned the hero around AI agents, platforms, and business operations.
- Kept the existing dark identity and introduced a light portfolio section for contrast and visual hierarchy.
- Expanded the two original capability groupings into three clearly scoped areas based on existing project content.
- Presented both delivered systems as challenge, build scope, and expandable implementation detail.
- Labeled interface-like project visuals as illustrative schematics, not client screenshots.
- Added a dedicated engineering standards section and a four-stage delivery approach.
- Reframed the founder section around accountability and existing experience.
- Added practical, keyboard-accessible FAQs.
- Replaced the unavailable contact form with direct email links.
- Added descriptive metadata, a branded social preview, a site icon, robots.txt, and sitemap.xml.

Existing founder achievements and delivery statements were retained from the supplied codebase. No new client names, certifications, testimonials, performance metrics, response-time guarantees, or team-size claims were invented.
